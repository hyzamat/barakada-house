03 — Inihaw

Fire is our oldest ingredient.

Every inasal, every bangus, every skewer of barbecue meets real charcoal. No gas, no shortcuts — just uling, smoke and someone who knows exactly when to turn it.

  • UlingLump charcoal, burned down to white-hot coals.
  • UsokCalamansi, lemongrass and achuete, kissed by smoke.
  • ApoyTurned by hand, charred to order.
